Forever Quest Journal 1.5.0

Complete English/French zone browser for WoW Forever.

Open with /fqj, /questjournal, the AddOn Compartment, or the draggable
minimap button.

Features
- DungeonJournal-inspired zone cards ordered by average quest level.
- Searchable two-column browser for every populated outdoor and city zone.
- Pan and zoom the quest tree to explore branches and standalone quests.
- Search highlights matching nodes. Press Enter to cycle through results.
- Cross-zone quest links open the destination tree. Back restores the
  previous view. Click the zone name to return to the gallery.
- Quest details open in a draggable panel that grows with its content.
- Zone-level summaries use the mean and median relevant quest levels instead
  of relying only on minimum/maximum outliers, rounded to whole levels. The
  traditional range appears beside the zone name.
- English and French interface, quest names, objectives, quest givers,
  prerequisites, and chain links.
- Compact flat language selector with clear English/French menu rows.
- Per-character quest states appear as colored rings, with checkmarks on
  finished quests and a legend beneath the tree.
- Quest states refresh live after quest-log changes and turn-ins.
- Quest lists and zone totals respect the current character's race and faction.
- Required quest chains and alternative prerequisites.
- Possible item rewards with localized names and in-game item tooltips.
- Quest-giver, object, and item start information.
- A cohesive custom icon set for quest details, rewards, chains, and active
  quests.
- "Show Start on Map" creates a user waypoint in the quest's actual starting
  zone whenever a coordinate is known.
- Opening a zone map displays every currently available quest start, grouping
  quests that share the same giver or location into one readable marker.
- A world-map toggle hides or restores all available-quest markers and remembers
  the setting for each character.
- Forever Quest Journal and Dungeon Journal can remain open together; the
  most recently opened journal is shown intact above the other.

Data notes
- Generated from QuestieDB's Forever data and frFR localization at revision
  4dbb82440a27856f8dccf57094a5d792cdc7cc9b.
- The generated database contains 3,977 quests in 48 populated zones.
- QuestieDB: https://github.com/Questie/QuestieDB
- The data may lag WoW Forever hotfixes.
- Every zone card uses a uniformly cropped panoramic landscape from Wowpedia's
  World of Warcraft Classic zone screenshot collection. Per-file source pages
  are recorded in Media/Zones/SOURCES.txt.
